# Design Taste

Your job is to **reject mediocre AI UI** and iterate until the product feels premium and intentional.

## Mandatory loop

1. Inspect existing UI (run app; screenshots if available).
2. Identify visual problems (clutter, weak hierarchy, template look).
3. Compare against high-quality modern products (Linear, Vercel, MDN — principles only).
4. Establish hierarchy (what is primary / secondary / ambient).
5. Remove unnecessary elements.
6. Improve spacing (consistent rhythm; less random padding).
7. Improve typography (scale, weight, tracking, measure).
8. Improve interaction (focus, hover, transport controls).
9. Check responsive behavior.
10. Visually inspect the final result.
11. Iterate — do not stop at the first pass.

## Cluster looks to avoid (unless brief demands)

- Purple-on-white / purple-indigo gradient themes
- Warm cream + terracotta + generic serif combo
- Broadsheet hairline newspaper layouts
- Near-black + acid green “hacker” default
- Glow/glass/card spam

## Done when

A senior designer would not immediately classify it as “AI-generated template.”
